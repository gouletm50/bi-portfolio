# Revenue Variance Analysis

## Business Question

**Why was April 2025 revenue 23% lower than April 2024?**

---

# Investigation 1 – Product Analysis

## Question 1 – Which products explain the revenue decline?

### Observation

Total revenue decreased from **$746,673** in April 2024 to **$573,810** in April 2025, a decline of **$172,863 (-23%)**.

Pump A200 revenue decreased from **$267,592** in April 2024 to **$77,306** in April 2025, a decline of **$190,286**.

### Finding

Pump A200 is the primary driver of the April 2025 revenue decline.

The decrease in Pump A200 revenue exceeded the company's total revenue decline, indicating that increases in other products partially offset the loss.

---

# Investigation 2 – Territory Analysis

## Question 1 – Was the Pump A200 decline limited to one territory?

### Observation

Pump A200 revenue declined across multiple territories between April 2024 and April 2025.

The largest declines occurred in:

- **Greater Toronto Area:** -$113,002
- **Eastern Ontario:** -$65,823
- **Montreal:** -$41,080

**Atlantic Canada** was the only territory showing growth, increasing by **$29,618** and partially offsetting the decline.

### Finding

The Pump A200 decline was not isolated to a single territory.

The decline was broad-based, with the largest impacts coming from Greater Toronto Area and Eastern Ontario. Atlantic Canada's growth partially offset the decline but was not sufficient to compensate for the losses in other territories.

---

# Investigation 3 – Customer Analysis

## Question 1 – Which customers drove the Pump A200 decline?

### Observation

Pump A200 purchasing changed significantly between April 2024 and April 2025 across several customers.

Customer-level analysis showed:

- **Ontario Steel Works** generated $62,736 in Pump A200 revenue in April 2024 but no Pump A200 revenue in April 2025.
- **Capital Infrastructure** generated $56,777 in Pump A200 revenue in April 2024 but no Pump A200 revenue in April 2025.
- **Quebec Industrial Systems** generated $41,080 in Pump A200 revenue in April 2024 but no Pump A200 revenue in April 2025.
- **Northern Manufacturing Ltd.** reduced April Pump A200 revenue substantially.
- **Precision Components Inc.** reduced April Pump A200 revenue moderately.
- **Atlantic Fabrication** generated new Pump A200 revenue in April 2025.

### Finding

The April 2025 Pump A200 revenue decline was driven by lower purchasing activity from several existing customers compared with April 2024.

However, additional analysis of annual Pump A200 purchases shows that some customers continued purchasing Pump A200 outside of April, suggesting that the decline may be related to purchase timing or order timing rather than permanent customer loss.

Although **Atlantic Fabrication generated new Pump A200 revenue in April 2025**, the additional sales were not sufficient to offset the revenue decline from existing customers.
